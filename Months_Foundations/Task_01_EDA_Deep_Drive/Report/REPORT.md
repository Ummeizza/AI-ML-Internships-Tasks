# 2. Business Problem

The telecom company is experiencing a high customer churn rate, resulting in significant revenue loss. The objective of this analysis is to identify which customer groups are most likely to churn and determine the factors contributing to customer attrition.

---

# 3. Data Overview

- Dataset: Telco Customer Churn
- Source: Kaggle
- Target Variable: Churn
- Features include customer demographics, services, contract type, payment method, tenure, and monthly charges.

---

- Data Cleaning
- Data Preprocessing

- Bivariate Analysis
- Multivariate Analysis
- Correlation Analysis
- Business Insights

---

# 5. Results & Key Findings

The analysis revealed that:

- Customers with month-to-month contracts have a higher churn rate.
- Customers with shorter tenure are more likely to churn.
- Customers with higher monthly charges tend to churn more frequently.
- Customers on longer contracts are more likely to stay with the company.

---

# 6. Limitations

- The dataset represents historical customer behaviour.
- Correlation does not necessarily imply causation.
- Additional business information could improve the analysis.

---

# 7. Recommendations

- Encourage customers to switch to longer-term contracts.
- Improve retention strategies for new customers.
- Offer personalised promotions to high-risk customers.
- Focus on improving customer satisfaction.
